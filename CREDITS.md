# Credits

## Photos

The photos in `backgrounds/` are not mine. They were taken by **SpaceX** and come from the official mission pages on [spacex.com](https://www.spacex.com). All rights to them stay with SpaceX. They are included here to celebrate Starship and SpaceX's support of Omarchy as a Founding Corporate Patron of the Omacom Foundation.

| File | Mission | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `1-starship-flight-14-launch.jpg` | Starship Flight 14, 28 September 2026, the first orbital flight | [spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-14](https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-14) |
| `2-starship-flight-13-on-the-pad.jpg` | Starship Flight 13 on the pad at Starbase | [spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-13](https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-13) |
| `3-starship-flight-12-liftoff.jpg` | Starship Flight 12, 22 May 2026, flying Starship V3 | [spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-12](https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-12) |

SpaceX publishes these at 2600x1200. For this theme they are cropped to 16:9 around the rocket, upscaled 4x with [Real-ESRGAN](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN) (`realesrgan-x4plus`) and scaled to 7680x4320 (8K).

If you are from SpaceX and want a photo changed or removed, open an issue and it will be done right away.
